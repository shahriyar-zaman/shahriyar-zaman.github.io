Hi! I study when language and vision-language models truly reason—and when they merely exploit shortcuts. I conduct large-scale evaluations of foundation models to uncover systematic limitations, understand why they fail, and develop mitigation strategies that improve their reliability, robustness, and efficiency.

**Grounded Multimodal Reasoning.** I evaluate whether vision-language models genuinely understand spatial, temporal, and compositional relationships in real-world settings. *Selected work:* **TimeSpot** [ICML 2026] · **SpatiaLab** [ICLR 2026] · **CAPSTONE** [EMNLP 2025]

**Formal and Symbolic Reasoning.** I investigate whether LLMs can construct executable computational systems—including pushdown automata and Turing machines—rather than merely describe them. *Selected work:* **From Language Specifications to Executable Turing Machines** [EMNLP 2026] · **Can LLMs Design Computational Machines?** [EMNLP 2026]

**Trustworthy Foundation-Model Evaluation.** I identify and mitigate limitations involving hallucination, adversarial vulnerability, cultural misalignment, and computational inefficiency. I am particularly interested in culturally grounded evaluations for Bengali and other underrepresented languages. *Selected work:* **BengaliMoralBench** [ACM FAccT 2026]

I am currently a **Research Intern at Vanderbilt University**, a **Research Assistant at the University of Dhaka**, and a **Research Collaborator with the Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI)**. I graduated **summa cum laude** in Computer Science and Engineering from **North South University**.

My research has appeared at **ICML, ICLR, EMNLP, and ACM FAccT**, and my earlier work received the **Best Paper Award at IEEE IS 2024**. I also contribute to academic service as a reviewer for **WACV, NeurIPS, ACL SRW,** and **ICML workshops**.
